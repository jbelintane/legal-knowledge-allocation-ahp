# Simulation Results

This directory contains the complete outputs of the simulations conducted for the monograph *Aplicação de Pesquisa Operacional para Administração de Tarefas e Conhecimento em um Departamento Jurídico*.

The simulations compare two task allocation approaches:

- **without_AHP**: baseline allocation method, primarily based on analyst specialization and workload.
- **with_AHP**: multicriteria allocation method based on the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP), incorporating learning, specialization, and workload as decision criteria.

## Experimental Design

Four demand scenarios were simulated:

1. **Base scenario** – baseline distribution of legal activities, with 5 tasks generated per business day.
2. **Concentrated scenario** – 50% of incoming tasks concentrated in the Corporate Law (`Societário`) area.
3. **Overload scenario** – workload increased to 15 tasks per business day.
4. **Concentrated + overload scenario** – combination of task concentration in Corporate Law and increased workload.

Each scenario was evaluated with teams of:

- 5 analysts
- 7 analysts
- 10 analysts

Therefore, 12 experimental configurations were evaluated under each allocation method, resulting in 24 simulation runs.

## Directory Structure

The results are divided into:

- `with_AHP/` – simulations using the AHP-based allocation model.
- `without_AHP/` – simulations using the baseline allocation model.

Each simulation directory contains five CSV files:

### `DB_WK.csv`

Complete task worklog generated during the simulation, containing the allocation history and execution information for simulated tasks.

### `df_concentracao_area.csv`

Knowledge concentration indicators calculated for each legal area.

### `df_conhecimento_analista_area.csv`

Distribution of accumulated knowledge among analysts and legal areas.

### `df_metricas.csv`

Consolidated simulation metrics.

### `estado_aprendizagem.csv`

Final learning state generated by the learning model for each analyst.

## Reproducibility

The datasets contained in this directory are outputs generated from synthetic data. No confidential information or data from an actual legal department are included.

The Python source code used to generate the simulations will be available in the `/code` directory.
